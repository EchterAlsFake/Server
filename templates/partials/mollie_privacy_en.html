Information sent to and received from Mollie

When you create a Mollie checkout, this server sends the price and currency, the description “Porn Fetch lifetime Premium license”, a generated order reference and the return and notification URLs. The description identifies this product to Mollie. The email entered on this website for license delivery, your country declaration, browsing history and downloaded-video records are not included in this payment-creation request. No Mollie script or embedded payment frame is loaded on this checkout page; payment is completed on Mollie's hosted page.

On opening the provider checkout, you communicate directly with Mollie and, as required by the selected method, participating banks or card providers. They may process payment, identity, device and connection information. This website does not collect your card number, card security code or online-banking credentials. Information you separately enter on Mollie's or your bank's pages is governed by their own notices; it is separate from the license-delivery email collected here.

I retrieve the provider's payment reference, profile, amount, currency, status and necessary refund or chargeback information to verify payment and handle disputes. Provider responses can contain further payer or payment-method details; the application processes responses in memory and does not persist them in full. Local payment records contain the order/provider references, amount, currency, selected country and country-verification evidence, checkout URL, pricing version, payment/reversal state, processing and retry timestamps, and license association. The full payment response, card details and banking login are not stored in these local records.

My purposes, legal bases and retention

I use these data to create and reconcile your order, prevent duplicate payment or license issuance, supply the license, handle refunds and support, and retain necessary evidence. My legal bases are Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the contract and requested pre-contract steps, Art. 6(1)(c) for statutory accounting and contract-confirmation duties, and Art. 6(1)(f) for security, reliable processing and defending legal claims. These payment data are not used by me for advertising. Without the required email, country and payment information, I cannot process this checkout.

The required delivery email is encrypted with the order. A separate encrypted queue copy is cleared after successful email delivery or, for abandoned unpaid checkouts, by the scheduled cleanup after seven days. Clearing the queue does not delete the order's email reference. Paid orders awaiting confirmation continue to be retried. Proton Mail receives the email and attachments for delivery. The email is not placed in your license or sent to Keygen. Order email references, consent evidence, confirmation PDFs and payment records are reviewed and deleted manually when contractual, claims and applicable statutory retention needs end; they have no automatic seven-day deletion rule. Section 12's accounting, email and backup retention rules also apply.

I record the accepted terms and product description, immediate-supply consent, acknowledgment concerning withdrawal, policy version and timestamp with the order, and provide the resulting confirmation with the license and receipt. No cryptocurrency refund consent is requested for Mollie. Private recovery links and necessary session cookies operate as described below. Single-order checkout grants expire after fifteen minutes; the payment worker removes their server records once they have been expired for seven days. Necessary rate-limit counters protect the forms. The optional tester-password form also uses a keyed IP-derived value for its short-lived rate limit, not the plain IP address in that counter.

The local country and Tor checks described in this policy also apply to Mollie checkout. Country evidence is stored with the order; connection IP addresses and subdivision results used for those checks are discarded. My application verifies payment state and geographic eligibility automatically; you can contact me for manual review if a purchase is blocked or its status appears incorrect. Mollie makes its own payment and fraud decisions. Its processing can involve recipients outside the EEA; its privacy statement explains the safeguards. I do not promise that all provider processing occurs solely within the EU.

10 installations. Your choice.

Each installation generates a random, unique ID. It is not derived from your hardware and does not identify you personally. The server uses it to count your active installations.

Each activated installation uses one slot. You can keep up to 10 installations active at the same time. To activate an eleventh, deactivate an existing installation in the app’s settings first.

A fresh installation with a new ID uses a new slot, even on the same device. You stay in control of which installations are active.

Need more slots for special circumstances? Email support@echteralsfake.me. I can raise your limit manually.

How to get your license

Patreon Shop is a one-time purchase. You only need a free Patreon account; no recurring membership is required.

After you have bought Porn Fetch Premium on Patreon, go to https://pornfetch.to/activate and enter the email address your Patreon account is associated with. Your license key will be sent to that email address.

Delivery can take up to 10 minutes. Check your Spam folder, especially if you use Gmail.

Patreon shows the final price, including applicable fees and taxes, before you buy.

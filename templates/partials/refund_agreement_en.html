Refund method

With your separate express consent, license refunds for NOWPayments and Banxa-funded purchases are paid in cryptocurrency. Patreon payments are refunded through Patreon to the original payment method within 90 days of payment; afterwards, cryptocurrency is agreed. This changes no statutory entitlement, amount or deadline, and does not reverse Banxa's separate cryptocurrency sale or restrict payment-provider disputes.

Before transfer, we agree the currency, network and your receiving address, preferably using the original currency/network. For fiat-denominated claims, the full amount owed is converted at an agreed, documented current rate. The supplier bears necessary network, receipt and conversion costs. If this cannot be completed on time without extra cost to you, the original payment method or another expressly agreed cost-free method is used; deadlines are unchanged.

Website consent is not automatically linked to Patreon. Without attributable consent, any alternative method is agreed individually. Prior refunds and payment disputes are checked to avoid duplicate payouts.

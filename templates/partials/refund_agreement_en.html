Separate agreement on the refund method

This agreement applies only with separate express consent and only to refunds owed by the supplier in connection with the Porn Fetch license. It creates no additional refund entitlement and does not limit statutory claims, amounts or deadlines.

NOWPayments purchases and license purchases funded through Banxa are refunded in cryptocurrency. Patreon payments are refunded through Patreon to the original payment method within 90 days of the respective original payment; after that, a cryptocurrency refund is agreed. Independent refunds or dispute procedures of Patreon, Apple, banks or payment providers remain unaffected. A license refund does not automatically reverse the separate cryptocurrency purchase from Banxa.

Before a cryptocurrency refund, I agree with you on the currency, network and a receiving address you control. Where possible, I use the cryptocurrency and network used for the license payment. A different cryptocurrency requires your consent. For a claim denominated in fiat currency, the full amount owed is converted using a documented current exchange rate agreed with you before transfer. Exchange-rate movements do not reduce the claim owed; this agreement does not prescribe a blanket refund of the original number of coins.

The supplier bears the necessary network, receipt and conversion costs of the agreed refund, so the alternative method causes you no additional costs. If the agreed cryptocurrency refund cannot be completed on time and without additional costs to you, the statutory repayment obligation remains: I use the original payment method or agree another cost-free option with you. These arrangements do not extend statutory refund deadlines.

Website consent is not automatically linked to a Patreon membership. Keep your checkout agreement receipt. For a direct Patreon purchase or where attributable evidence is missing, I agree an alternative refund method with you individually. Membership or a webhook alone does not constitute consent. Existing refunds and payment disputes are checked before an additional payout; this does not exclude your statutory rights.

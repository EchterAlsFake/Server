When your year starts
Your license covers releases published within 1 year of first activation, not the date you buy.
What you keep
Keep Premium in eligible releases afterwards. Activation and periodic online validation still apply.
After your covered year
App updates stay free. Premium in later releases requires renewed entitlement. No automatic renewal.
Where you can use it
Up to 10 active installations. Deactivate an installation in the app before adding another when all slots are in use.

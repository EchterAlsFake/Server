At checkout, you are asked to select your country so that the place of supply can be documented for tax purposes and geographic restrictions can be applied. When you submit the checkout form, my server temporarily processes the connection IP address in memory and compares its country with your selection using a locally installed DB-IP City Lite database. Only the selected country name, the fact that customer declaration and local IP geolocation agreed, and the database release label are retained with the transaction. No street, billing, or other address is requested or stored for this check. DB-IP City Lite is provided by DB-IP.com under CC BY 4.0.

NowPayments acts as an independent controller for its payment processing. My server stores the order and provider identifiers, payment status, invoice details, amount, currency, transaction hash, selected country and country-evidence metadata needed to verify and document a purchase. The cryptocurrency checkout does not require a name or email address; an email address supplied for the optional card-assisted flow is passed to the payment provider but is not retained by my server. Please note that blockchain transactions are public by nature and can be viewed by anyone on the respective blockchain network. For more information about how NowPayments handles your data, please refer to NowPayments' Privacy Policy. For data-related inquiries to NowPayments, contact: support@nowpayments.io.

Patreon members may restrict email sharing; automatic email delivery is then unavailable. Patreon provides member data under its Creator Privacy Promise. For more information, see Patreon's Privacy Policy and Creator Privacy Promise.

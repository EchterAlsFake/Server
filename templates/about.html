I’m Johannes Habel, the developer and maintainer of Porn Fetch.

Why I build Porn Fetch

I build tools that let you save supported videos and collections to your own device, with control over quality, file names, concurrency, and privacy settings. Porn Fetch provides a graphical app and a separate command-line interface.

Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android are supported, with package availability listed on the download page. iOS is limited to the jailbroken iPhone CLI setup.

04 / Privacy

Personal content.
Personal control.

Porn Fetch runs locally. Browsing history and download records are not sent to the developer’s server. Choose the connection controls that work for you.

Encrypted DNS & ECH
Configure primary and fallback DNS over HTTPS resolvers. Encrypted Client Hello depends on support from the network and destination.
Tor & proxies
Route app connections through Tor and configure onion routing for license and update checks. Proxy connections are supported too.
Anonymous mode
Conceal the app’s presentation from people looking at your screen. This is a local display feature.
SNI obfuscation
Optional connection controls with platform and privilege requirements. Destination IP addresses may remain visible.

Independent by design

A private app.
A transparent server.

The app’s full source is available to inspect. The server and payment infrastructure are open source under the GPL.

No IP logs. No viewing-history logs.
No visitor IP addresses are stored by the Porn Fetch server. The app does not send your browsing or download history to it.
Self-hosted at home in Germany.
Independent hardware in the developer’s home runs the service. A Swedish relay forwards encrypted connections to Germany; HTTPS terminates on the German endpoint.
Tor routing supported.
Use the app’s Tor connection settings and visit this website through its onion service. External payment providers have their own network and identity requirements.
Privacy-friendly crypto checkout.
Buy through NOWPayments without a Porn Fetch account. Network and provider fees apply; cryptocurrency payments are not necessarily anonymous.
No ads or advertising trackers.
No advertising networks, cross-site profiling or third-party analytics. Aggregate service counters, necessary session cookies and purchase records are described in the privacy policy.

Privacy controls offer different protections; none guarantees anonymity. Websites, network operators, licensing and payment services have their own data handling.

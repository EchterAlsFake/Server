How to verify your download

Expand advanced options and use the SHA-256 link beside your exact file. Compare it with the digest calculated on your device.

Windows PowerShell: Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 <filename>
Linux: sha256sum <filename>
macOS: shasum -a 256 <filename>

A checksum detects a changed or incomplete file; it is not a substitute for a code-signing certificate.

Which architecture do I need?

x64 means Intel/AMD 64-bit. ARM64 includes Apple Silicon and compatible ARM PCs. Android APKs identify their ABI separately: arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86, or x86_64. Use your system’s device information rather than guessing from the browser.

How do I check a download?

Compare your file’s SHA-256 digest with the checksum linked next to the download. A checksum detects a changed or incomplete file; it is not a substitute for a code-signing certificate.

Windows PowerShell: Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 <filename>
Linux: sha256sum <filename>
macOS: shasum -a 256 <filename>

Download a {{ guide.name }} video on your computer

  1. Install Porn Fetch. Choose your Windows, macOS, or Linux package. Follow that platform’s installation guide and open the app.
  2. Copy the video’s page URL. Open the individual {{ guide.name }} video you are authorized to save in your browser and copy the complete page address.
  3. Fetch the video. In the app’s Downloads page, paste the address into Video URL and choose Get Video. Wait for its details to appear in the list.
  4. Choose a quality and folder. Use the video row’s quality control to select an available stream. Check Output Path in Settings before starting. Free downloads are limited to available qualities up to 720p.
  5. Start the download. Choose Download on the video row and wait for completion. Open your configured output folder to find the saved file.

Fetching loads the video into the list; it does not start the download. For several entries, review and select the rows first, then use Download Selected.

Downloading on Android

Install a compatible APK from the Android downloads page. Open Add videos, choose Video URL, paste the complete {{ guide.name }} address, and tap Get Video.

Choose a quality on the returned video card and tap Download. The add-video panel shows Output folder and a Choose folder control. Completed files are saved to your selected folder automatically; there is no separate per-video Save action. Use your file manager to open the saved file.

Using the command line

Set up the Porn Fetch CLI for your system and open its interactive menu. Choose Download Single URL for an individual video, paste its full address, and follow the prompts. Use Settings & Configuration to check your output folder and preferred quality.

The video loads, but I cannot choose the quality I want

Choose from the streams returned for that video. Qualities above 720p require a valid Porn Fetch license, and the source must expose a usable stream. Premium does not upscale videos or remove website-side restrictions. Read the license activation guide if your purchased entitlement is not active.

A profile or playlist is empty

Check the URL type, Max Result Limit in Settings, and any filters in Advanced Configuration. Free use previews the first five videos of a public model, channel, or playlist before filters are applied; a license is needed for the full collection. Account collections are exempt from this preview. Duration, quality, date, and text filters can remove entries. Some pages also depend on region or account access. Only use the profile and playlist workflows supported for {{ guide.name }}.

The video still fails

Check your app version against the release notes, confirm that the page is accessible in your browser, and try fetching it again. Website changes can break support. If it continues, contact support with the app version, operating system, and exact error. Keep passwords, session tokens, and cookies out of your message.

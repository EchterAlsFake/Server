Before your first download

Install the app for Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, or iOS 15+ with a native GUI. A command-line interface is also available.

Free use supports available qualities up to 720p, one active download and the first five videos of each public profile, model, channel or playlist. Account collections are exempt from the five-video preview. App updates and desktop download history and statistics are free. Premium unlocks higher qualities when available, parallel downloads and full public collections. Some YouPorn MP4-only videos also require a license because their resolution is unknown. Keyword search is unavailable: use a full supported page URL.

For adults 18+. Only save content you are authorized to download. Porn Fetch is independent of the listed websites; support can change when a website changes.

Before your first download

Install the app for Windows, Linux, macOS, or Android. A command-line interface is also available. iPhone support is limited to the jailbroken iPhone CLI setup.

The free tier downloads available qualities up to 720p. Higher qualities need a Porn Fetch license and an available source stream. Some YouPorn MP4-only videos also require a license because their resolution is unknown. Keyword search is unavailable: use a full supported page URL.

For adults 18+. Only save content you are authorized to download. Porn Fetch is independent of the listed websites; support can change when a website changes.

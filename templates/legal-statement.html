Legal Statement

Updated: 1 November 2026. These notes explain the intended lawful use of Porn Fetch under German law. They are not a blanket finding that every download or access method is lawful. Users must have the necessary rights; the supplier's own legal obligations remain unaffected.

Technical basis

Porn Fetch runs on the user's device. It retrieves media from supported third-party services using supplied video, profile or collection URLs, including batch downloads. Certain account functions use user-provided credentials or browser cookies. Keyword search is disabled in the current 3.9 application. This website distributes the software; the ordinary download workflow does not relay the user's downloaded videos through the Porn Fetch application server.

1. Copyright & private copying

German law permits certain individual copies made by a natural person for private, non-commercial use, provided the source was not obviously unlawfully created or made publicly accessible. Public availability alone is not permission to copy or redistribute a video. Copies made under this exception must not be used for public distribution or public playback. Authorised downloads may also be possible under permission from the relevant rights holder. See § 53 UrhG.

The app handles media URLs and streams exposed by its supported integrations. A stream's format or technical accessibility does not establish its legal status. Transport encryption such as HTTPS is distinct from copyright access protection. The private-copying exception does not generally authorise bypassing effective technical protection measures; § 95a UrhG must be considered separately.

2. Database rights

A profile or playlist is not automatically an insignificant part of a platform's database. The amount and qualitative importance of the material matter. Repeated, systematic extraction of individually small parts may also infringe database rights when it conflicts with normal exploitation or unreasonably harms the database producer's legitimate interests. Porn Fetch supports batch retrieval and does not enforce a legal extraction quota. Users must assess their intended scope and permissions. See § 87b UrhG.

3. Access rights and protective measures

The application supports account sessions, browser-like networking and optional proxy/SNI-fragmentation settings. Its networking dependency also handles certain anti-bot challenges. It would therefore be inaccurate to claim that the software never interacts with access restrictions. Cookies demonstrate a technical session, not necessarily the user's legal entitlement to access or copy particular data.

§ 202a StGB concerns unauthorised access to data not intended for the person and specially secured against unauthorised access, obtained by overcoming that protection. Whether a particular restriction and method meet these conditions requires assessment of the actual circumstances. Neither every automated request nor every network workaround is automatically a criminal offence. Use only accounts and content you are entitled to access; the app does not grant permission to circumvent legally protected restrictions.

4. Competition law and platform terms

Client-side automation is not inherently unlawful, but its purpose and effects matter. The BGH decision I ZR 154/16 (Werbeblocker II, 19 April 2018) concerns an advertising blocker. It is not a general authorisation for scraping or downloading from third-party platforms.

Whether platform terms form part of a contract depends on the way the contract is concluded and the terms are incorporated, including notice, an opportunity to read them and agreement where § 305(2) BGB applies. Registration or its absence alone does not settle this. Accounts may already be subject to accepted terms. Copyright, database rights and other applicable laws remain relevant even without an enforceable contractual scraping prohibition.

5. Platform names and trademarks

Platform names identify the services with which an integration is intended to work. Referential use can be permitted under § 23(1) no. 3 and (2) MarkenG, subject to honest commercial practices. This is not a general permission to use every logo or presentation. Porn Fetch is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the named video platforms.

6. Protection of minors

Porn Fetch is intended for adults. Its local operation and the absence of a supplier-hosted video catalogue are relevant characteristics, but do not by themselves establish an exemption from all youth-protection obligations. The actual content and functionality of the website and application must be considered, including any previews or ways of making content accessible.

Where an online offering makes pornographic content available, the requirements for closed user groups under § 4(2) JMStV can apply. A simple “I am 18” declaration is not equivalent to the age verification required for such a group. Obligations of third-party platforms do not automatically discharge obligations of this offering. See the KJM guidance on restricted content and age verification. This statement does not assert that a closed user group is required solely because an app is a local downloader.

Installing the app

Start with your platform’s guide: Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, restricted iOS CLI, or CLI.

My download will not open

Check the operating system and architecture, extract the complete archive, and keep the bundled files together. Compare its SHA-256 checksum if the download may be incomplete. On Linux or macOS, check executable permissions. Your system’s security tools may ask you to review an unfamiliar application.

Should I use a Qt setup runner?

Qt setup runners install the app and provide a maintenance tool. They are experimental. Portable packages are the default download; keep them as a fallback if setup fails.

Where are older versions?

The release history keeps published versions and their notes. Old versions can have different features, requirements, and license terms; downloading an older version does not change its license.

Downloading a video

Find your website in the video download guides for the app’s actual steps, supported URL types, and provider-specific limits. Start with PornHub, XHamster, or XVideos.

Activating Premium

After verified payment, save your license file and invoice from the payment status page. If you requested email delivery, the license is also sent to your delivery address.

Open the app’s settings, choose Activate License, and select porn_fetch.license. Your year of updates starts at first activation. Activation and periodic validation still apply afterwards.

Is this a subscription?

No. A Porn Fetch license is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal. Versions released within your update year remain eligible; newer versions require another deliberate purchase. Patreon membership has its own recurring billing until cancelled, as explained before selecting that route.

Can I use the app offline?

Normal activation and periodic online validation apply. See the license terms for the current offline and update entitlement conditions.

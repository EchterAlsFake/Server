Installing the app

Start with your platform’s guide: Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, iOS 15+ native app, or CLI.

My download will not open

Check the operating system and architecture, extract the complete archive, and keep the bundled files together. Compare its SHA-256 checksum if the download may be incomplete. On Linux or macOS, check executable permissions. Your system’s security tools may ask you to review an unfamiliar application.

Should I use a Qt setup runner?

Qt installers are the recommended desktop download. Android uses APK packages; iOS 15+ uses a native IPA installed through a sideloading tool. Choose exactly which components to install; QMaintenanceTool manages components and app updates afterwards. The app offers update checks; you choose whether to install an update. Portable packages are an alternative that requires manually downloading and installing updates.

Where are older versions?

The release history keeps published versions and their notes. Old versions can have different features, requirements, and license terms; downloading an older version does not change its license.

Free and Premium features

Free use supports available qualities up to 720p, one active download and the first five videos of each public profile, model, channel or playlist. Account collections are exempt from that preview.

PAYMENT BRAND ARTWORK AND USAGE NOTES
Reviewed against the public owner guidelines on 24 October 2026.

The artwork below is served locally, without third-party image requests. Original
artwork, colors and aspect ratios are retained. Marks identify the referenced
payment provider or cryptocurrency; they do not imply sponsorship, endorsement,
affiliation, guaranteed acceptance or a working production checkout. The website's
Patreon and card routes are test previews. External services may still take money.

Each owner's current brand requirements and applicable provider agreement govern.
